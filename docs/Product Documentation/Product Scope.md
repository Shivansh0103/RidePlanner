# Ride Planner - Product Scope

## Introduction

Ride Planner is designed to simplify the planning and management of road trips.

This document defines the responsibilities of the platform, establishes clear product boundaries, and provides guidance for evaluating future features.

The scope described here represents the current direction of the product and may evolve as Ride Planner matures. Any future expansion should continue to align with the project's vision and product principles.

> **Guiding Principle:** Own the planning experience. Integrate everything else.

---

## Core Responsibility

Ride Planner is responsible for helping travelers:

* Plan journeys
* Organize trip information
* Prepare for travel
* Collaborate with travel companions
* Track important travel information
* Preserve meaningful trip memories

Every feature should contribute to one or more of these responsibilities.

---

## Core Domains

### Trip Planning

Planning and organizing trips from start to finish.

Examples include:

* Trip creation
* Multi-day itineraries
* Destinations and stops
* Daily schedules
* Trip templates
* Trip duplication and archival

---

### Route Planning

Helping travelers organize and understand their journey.

Examples include:

* Route planning
* Waypoints
* Scenic routes
* Alternative routes
* Distance and duration estimates
* GPX import and export (future)

Ride Planner organizes routes but does not replace dedicated navigation applications.

---

### Budget Management

Helping travelers estimate, manage, and review trip expenses.

Examples include:

* Fuel estimation
* Toll estimation
* Accommodation budgeting
* Expense tracking
* Shared expenses
* Budget summaries

---

### Travel Preparation

Helping travelers prepare before the journey begins.

Examples include:

* Packing lists
* Vehicle preparation checklists
* Travel documents
* Emergency contacts
* Personal notes

---

### Collaboration

Making trip planning easier for groups.

Examples include:

* Shared trips
* Trip invitations
* Collaborative itinerary planning
* Shared expense management
* Group planning

---

### Trip Memories

Helping travelers preserve the story of each journey.

Ride Planner manages the context of memories rather than acting as a cloud storage platform.

Examples include:

* Trip notes
* Timeline
* Places visited
* Trip highlights
* References to photo albums
* References to videos

Photo and video storage should be provided by dedicated storage providers or services whenever possible.

---

## Integrations

Ride Planner is designed to complement existing travel services rather than replace them.

Potential integrations include:

* Navigation applications
* Maps providers
* Weather services
* Calendar providers
* Cloud storage platforms
* Photo libraries
* Booking platforms

Ride Planner should become the central place where trip information is organized while allowing users to continue using the tools they already trust.

---

## Outside the Current Scope

The following responsibilities intentionally belong to specialized platforms:

* Turn-by-turn navigation
* Hotel and accommodation booking
* Flight booking
* Photo and video cloud storage
* Messaging applications
* Social networking
* Ride hailing
* Food delivery

These capabilities may be integrated where appropriate but are not intended to become core responsibilities of Ride Planner.

---

## Evaluating New Features

Before introducing a new feature, consider the following questions:

* Does it simplify road trip planning?
* Does it solve a genuine user problem?
* Does it align with Ride Planner's core responsibilities?
* Can an existing service already perform this task better?
* Should Ride Planner own this capability or integrate with another service?

If a feature cannot clearly answer these questions, it should be reconsidered before becoming part of the product.

---

## Scope Evolution

The scope of Ride Planner will continue to evolve as the product grows.

New capabilities should strengthen the platform without compromising its primary purpose: helping travelers plan, organize, and manage road trips more effectively.

As the product matures, this document should be reviewed and updated to reflect new insights, changing user needs, and lessons learned during development.
