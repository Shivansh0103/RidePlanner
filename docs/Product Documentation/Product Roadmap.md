# Ride Planner - Product Roadmap

## Introduction

The Product Roadmap describes the long-term evolution of Ride Planner from a core trip planning application into a comprehensive road trip planning platform.

Rather than defining fixed release dates or implementation schedules, the roadmap outlines the major milestones in the product's growth. Each milestone builds upon the previous one, gradually expanding the capabilities of Ride Planner while remaining aligned with the Project Vision, Product Scope, and Core Experience.

The roadmap is intended to communicate direction rather than deadlines.

---

## Roadmap Principles

The roadmap is guided by the following principles:

* Deliver meaningful user value with every milestone.
* Build upon a stable and reliable foundation.
* Improve existing experiences before introducing entirely new capabilities.
* Prefer depth over breadth.
* Expand thoughtfully without compromising the product's core purpose.
* Remain flexible as user needs and product understanding evolve.

---

# Product Evolution

Ride Planner is expected to evolve through the following milestones.

---

## Milestone 1 — Deliver the Core Experience

### Objective

Deliver the complete Core Experience defined in the **Core Experience** document.

The first milestone focuses on enabling travelers to successfully plan and manage an entire road trip using Ride Planner.

### Primary Product Domains

* Trip Management
* Itinerary Management
* Route Planning
* Budget & Expense Management
* Travel Preparation
* Trip Memories

### Primary Personas

* Solo Explorer
* Family Planner

### Success Criteria

Travelers can:

* Create and manage trips.
* Organize itineraries.
* Estimate and track expenses.
* Prepare before departure.
* Complete and archive trips.

The application should provide a complete planning experience without relying on spreadsheets or multiple note-taking applications.

---

## Milestone 2 — Improve the Planning Experience

### Objective

Reduce planning effort while improving organization and convenience.

This milestone introduces supporting capabilities that make planning more efficient without significantly increasing complexity.

### Primary Product Domains

* Accommodation
* Weather
* Notifications
* Templates
* Budget & Expense Management

### Primary Personas

* Family Planner
* Adventure Traveler

### Potential Capabilities

* Accommodation planning
* Weather forecasts
* Smart reminders
* Trip templates
* Improved expense summaries
* Better planning workflows

### Success Criteria

Planning becomes faster, more organized, and requires less manual effort.

---

## Milestone 3 — Enable Collaborative Planning

### Objective

Support travelers planning journeys together.

The focus shifts from individual planning to shared planning experiences.

### Primary Product Domains

* Collaboration
* Budget & Expense Management
* Notifications

### Primary Personas

* Group Organizer

### Potential Capabilities

* Shared trips
* Trip invitations
* Collaborative itinerary editing
* Shared expenses
* Role-based permissions

### Success Criteria

Groups can comfortably plan and manage trips together from a shared workspace.

---

## Milestone 4 — Build a Connected Experience

### Objective

Connect Ride Planner with the travel services users already rely on.

Ride Planner becomes the central hub that organizes information while integrating with specialized platforms.

### Primary Product Domains

* Integrations

### Primary Personas

* All Personas

### Potential Capabilities

* Calendar integration
* Navigation integration
* Cloud storage integration
* Photo library integration
* Booking references
* Import and export

### Success Criteria

Travelers can organize information from multiple services within a single planning experience.

---

## Milestone 5 — Deliver Intelligent Assistance

### Objective

Help travelers make better planning decisions with less effort.

The focus is on providing recommendations and automation while ensuring users remain in control of their trips.

### Primary Product Domains

* Intelligent Assistance

### Primary Personas

* All Personas

### Potential Capabilities

* Personalized recommendations
* Smart itinerary suggestions
* Packing suggestions
* Budget insights
* Route recommendations

### Success Criteria

Planning becomes more efficient through intelligent recommendations without reducing user control.

---

## Milestone 6 — Grow the Platform

### Objective

Expand Ride Planner into a broader ecosystem for road travelers.

The platform begins supporting knowledge sharing, inspiration, and community-driven planning.

### Primary Product Domains

* Community
* Analytics

### Primary Personas

* All Personas

### Potential Capabilities

* Community routes
* Public trips
* Travel inspiration
* Travel achievements
* Personal analytics
* Annual travel summaries

### Success Criteria

Ride Planner evolves beyond a planning application into a platform that travelers continue using before, during, and after every journey.

---

## Long-Term Direction

The long-term ambition is for Ride Planner to become the preferred starting point whenever someone begins planning a road trip.

As the product evolves, every milestone should strengthen the planning experience while preserving the principles of simplicity, practicality, flexibility, and reliability established in the Project Vision.

The roadmap should evolve alongside the product and remain responsive to user feedback, technological advancements, and lessons learned throughout development.
